Titles editor

Three ways to make a family's own words in the app (TODO.md F9), shown with the Gujarati words. Try the chips: ☆ makes a word the usual one, × removes it, and typing in “Add” and pressing Enter adds one. Pick one, or combine C with A or B.

A. Edit mode in the Family titles sheet

The sheet you already have gets an “Edit words” switch. Each relationship becomes a row of word chips you can change right there.

  • Nothing new to find: choosing and editing are in one place
  • Good for small changes to Gujarati or Hindi
  • Long to scroll through when building a whole new language

B. A full-screen editor

“Edit words” opens a screen of its own: the groups down the side with how many have words, a search, and room for long lists.

  • Best for filling in a whole new language, with progress you can see
  • Search finds “sister-in-law” among 62 relationships
  • One more screen to learn and to keep working on phones

C. A new language, step by step

“+ New language” starts a short flow: name it, copy words to start from, fill in the gaps group by group, then save. Editing it later uses A or B.

  • Makes a whole new language feel doable, one group at a time
  • Starting from Hindi or Gujarati means most rows are already filled
  • Only for new languages, so it comes with A or B
